# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students hunting hackathons — often between classes or late at night, scanning multiple platforms and worried they will miss the right event. Primary job: never miss a perfect hackathon for them.

## Product Purpose

Hackfind is a personal agent that discovers hackathons from multiple platforms, filters them against saved preferences, notifies across chosen channels, and tracks deadlines for joined or bookmarked events. Success looks like relevant opportunities reaching the student in time, with no duplicate noise, managed from a single dashboard. Built as a personal tool first, structured as a product from day one.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, accurate, personalized. Voice is direct and specific — no fluff, no hype. The product should feel like a precise filter that knows what they care about, not a marketplace shouting for attention.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS templates (purple gradients, identical feature-card grids, stock hero layouts). Cluttered hackathon boards and neon “hacker” aesthetic. Anything that could be swapped onto another student tool without changing the copy.

## Design Principles

- **Never miss** — prioritize timely, relevant signal over volume; accuracy beats completeness theater.
- **Personal by default** — preferences and matches should feel tailored, not one-size-fits-all browsing.
- **Sharp and direct** — one clear job per screen; primary actions obvious without explanation.
- **Product-grade from day one** — structure, clarity, and polish as if this ships to many students, even while personal.
- **Refuse the template** — every surface should feel specific to Hackfind; if it could belong to any startup, redesign it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast as default. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No special accommodations beyond solid inclusive defaults for now.
